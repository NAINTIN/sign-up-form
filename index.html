Persona 3 Reload

Persona 3 Reload is a remake of the 2006 JRPG Persona 3, developed by Atlus. You play as a high school student who can summon a Persona — a manifestation of your inner self — to fight Shadows during the "Dark Hour," a hidden 25th hour of the day. The game blends dungeon crawling in a tower called Tartarus with social simulation elements during the day, where you build relationships called Social Links that strengthen your Personas. It was released in February 2024 with fully modernized graphics, a reworked UI, new voice acting, and quality-of-life improvements while staying faithful to the original story.
